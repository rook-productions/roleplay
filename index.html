WHO WE ARE

Some propagate war. We propagate peace.

Our volunteers travel the globe looking for opportunities to help nations that have been negatively impacted by conflict, providing supplies, resources, and provisions.

Many will see the peace symbol and our pacifist ways and assume that we are weak, or that we are an easy target. But those people underestimate the influence of soft power and the sort of alliances that are born of gratitude, as opposed to those formed from necessity or practicality.

And remember: There's more to the Peace Corps than meets the Eye.

ROLEPLAY

Your goal as a roleplayer in the Peace Corps is to be helpful, friendly, and to endear as many people to you and the Peace Corps as possible.

Gravitate toward the smaller groups more likely to be oppressed to make allies, or to the larger, more oppressive groups if you want to be an unassuming spy to gather intelligence.

RULES

The event rules that will get you banned. Moderation is at the admins' discretion, so do not test the edges.

  • Story first. Ruining content (meetings, gatherings, big moments) is a ban.
  • No stream sniping, at any level. Watching an in-event creator's stream while you are on the server counts. Twitch Drops come from non-event streams or the post-event window.
  • No targeting streamers to annoy them or to farm screentime. Obvious malice toward creators means removal from the event, and it is one of the fastest ways to be banned from all future events with no appeal.
  • No cheating or third-party assistance, including map and plugin exploits.
  • No insiding, ghosting, loot-running, or griefing. No walling off monuments (quarries exempt).
  • No pre-event alliances with other countries. The story forms in-game.
  • No going AFK (that draws a kick), no hate speech, no copyrighted music.
